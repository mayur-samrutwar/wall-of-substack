WALL OF SUBSTACK

Double-click index.html to open your board. No installation needed.

Drag a clipping to move it. Click it to read the original article.
Pin a clipping adds a quote and its article link. Use the pencil to edit
and the × to remove. You can also drag a clipping into the dustbin.
Undo is available briefly after removal.

The download button on each clipping saves a PNG with the quote and
source credit for sharing.

Your clippings and positions save automatically in this browser.
Use the same browser and folder location to keep accessing your wall.

Keyboard: Tab to a clipping, Enter to read, arrow keys to move.
Hold Shift with an arrow key to move farther.

The board works offline using fallback fonts. Article links and the
optional Google Fonts need internet.
